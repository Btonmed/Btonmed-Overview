# Btonmed Website

**Innovation · Skills · Healthier Tomorrow**

The official website of **Btonmed**, a biomedical equipment marketplace and engineering services company.
Btonmed helps healthcare facilities access reliable medical technology and keep it running, and trains the
next generation of biomedical engineers.

## What Btonmed offers

- **Medical equipment sales:** imaging, diagnostic and theatre equipment
- **Equipment leasing:** flexible ways for facilities to access the technology they need
- **Servicing and repairs:** keeping critical equipment working
- **Maintenance and calibration:** keeping equipment accurate and safe
- **Technical support and engineering:** installation, commissioning and user training
- **Biomedical Engineering Bootcamp:** hands-on training for aspiring biomedical engineers
